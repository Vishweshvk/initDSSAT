Independent outcome context

Real-world crop outcomes

DES Area, Production & Yield reports are shown as regional context. Their district/state averages are not field-level labels and are never used to train the residual model.

Interpret carefully. A district or state yield summarizes many farms and conditions. This page does not score individual DSSAT runs against those aggregates or add them to paired training data.

Source and import

Choose the state/district, crop, season, and year report from the official DES APY Reports portal. If a CSV or supported Excel export is offered, save it under data/external/des_apy/downloads/. The OGD alternative below also provides a CSV/ZIP download. If the report has a yield column, specify its printed unit:

python -m cli.main import-des-apy --source "data/external/des_apy/downloads/report.csv" --yield-unit kg/ha --area-unit "1000 ha" --production-unit "1000 tonnes"

If the report has area and production but no yield column, omit --yield-unit; the importer derives yield only when you provide the source units, using production (tonnes) × 1000 / area (ha).

An alternative is the OGD district-wise, season-wise crop production dataset, which describes area in hectares and production in tonnes; check its coverage for your selected years before importing.

The importer records the source URL and retrieval time, preserves source units, normalizes or derives yield in kg/ha, flags invalid rows, and writes separate context-only files under data/external/des_apy/processed/. It does not alter the DSSAT paired dataset or model.
