Candidate datasets — not integrated into training

Counts and descriptions below are supplied in the project’s current research brief. Verify the original files, variable definitions, access conditions, and provenance before using these values as project data. Candidate record counts must not be added until overlap is resolved.

Candidate A5,689 × 49CIMMYT eastern India dataset · six seasons · ten districts (reported summary)
Candidate B3,735 × 61Earlier sowing-date trial dataset · five seasons (reported summary)

Neither candidate dataset has been incorporated into the current 28-row installed-example DSSAT benchmark or model artifacts. No sowing-time forecast is produced from them.

Open data-audit issues

  • Likely record overlap between candidates; counts are not additive.
  • Coordinate inconsistencies among candidate matching records.
  • Conflicting units for seed and fertilizer quantities.
  • A small number of date discrepancies need source-level review.
  • Measured soil profiles are missing.
  • Irrigation amounts and urea application dates are missing.
  • The supplied tables do not include a complete daily weather series.
  • No repeated early-season crop-observation time series is available for validating day-30/day-60 updates.

These gaps affect what can be represented by DSSAT, what is known at each forecast date, and how independent validation must be designed. Resolve them before claiming field-level forecast accuracy.
