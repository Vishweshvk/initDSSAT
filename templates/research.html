Research goals not yet demonstrated

  • Sowing-time and day-30/day-60 forecasts need weather information available at each forecast date and repeated, time-stamped crop observations.
  • Independent field-level measured yields are needed to establish performance beyond DSSAT example experiments.
  • The 90%-within-±10% field-prediction target is unachieved. Calibrated uncertainty estimates are also not available.
  • GeoNames coordinates identify a settlement, and NASA POWER is gridded weather. Neither is an exact farm measurement.
  • The selected broad soil-texture value remains context. A complete nearby DSSAT profile may be used; otherwise the source experiment's soil profile is retained.
